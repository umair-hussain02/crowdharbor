You are a senior professional UI/UX designer, conversion-focused web designer, and product design strategist.

Design a premium, modern, strategic **Pricing Page** for a company called **CrowdHarbor**.

CrowdHarbor helps early-stage founders prepare before fundraising by diagnosing funding readiness, reviewing key fundraising materials, identifying blockers, recommending the right capital pathway, and helping founders create a clear action plan before approaching investors, crowdfunding platforms, grants, accelerators, or other capital sources.

The Pricing Page must communicate this core idea:

**Choose the right preparation path before you raise.**

The page should help founders compare options clearly and confidently without making the pricing feel cheap, overly salesy, or like a generic SaaS subscription table.

The most important message:

**The Capital Pathway Sprint is the recommended main offer.**

The page should make founders feel:

**“I can start with a readiness review, but if I am serious about raising soon, the Capital Pathway Sprint is the right next step.”**

# 19. FAQ Section

## Purpose

Answer pricing and buying objections.

## Section Copy

Heading:

**Pricing questions before you start.**

Use accordion FAQ cards.

## FAQ Items

1. **Which plan should I start with?**
   If you are unsure where you stand, start with the Funding Readiness Review. If you are preparing to raise soon and need deeper material review and pathway strategy, choose the Capital Pathway Sprint.

2. **Why is the Capital Pathway Sprint recommended?**
   Because it goes beyond a score. It includes readiness diagnosis, pitch review, financial readiness review, data room checklist, strategy session, pathway recommendation, and action roadmap.

3. **Can I upgrade after the Readiness Review?**
   Yes, the Readiness Review can act as the first step before moving into the Sprint or deeper preparation support.

4. **Is the Preparation Program paid upfront?**
   The Preparation Program should be application-based. Payment details can be confirmed after reviewing the founder’s needs and fit.

5. **Do you guarantee funding?**
   No. CrowdHarbor helps founders prepare before approaching funding sources. It does not guarantee funding.

6. **Are you a broker or investor?**
   No. CrowdHarbor is not an investor, broker, funding guarantor, or crowdfunding platform.

7. **What payment method do you accept?**
   For launch, payment should be handled through Stripe checkout.

8. **What happens after I pay?**
   You complete the intake, upload relevant materials, and CrowdHarbor begins the review process based on your selected offer.

9. **What if I do not have a pitch deck yet?**
   You can still start with a readiness review. The process can identify what materials are missing and what should be prepared first.

10. **Can I get a refund?**
    Refund terms should be clearly defined in the Terms page. Since this is a service-based review process, refund rules should be transparent before payment.

Open animation: height + opacity

FAQ behavior:
