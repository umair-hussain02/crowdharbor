# Task: Create Three Separate CrowdHarbor Legal Pages and Link Them in Footer

Please create **three separate legal pages** for CrowdHarbor. Do not combine them into one page.

The three pages should be:

1. **Privacy Policy**
2. **Terms of Service**
3. **Disclaimer**

Each page must have its own route, its own page title, and its own footer link.

---

# Required Page Routes

Create these routes:

* `/privacy-policy`
* `/terms-of-service`
* `/disclaimer`

Footer links should point to these exact pages:

* Privacy Policy → `/privacy-policy`
* Terms of Service → `/terms-of-service`
* Disclaimer → `/disclaimer`

---

# Footer Requirement

Add a **Legal** column in the website footer.

Footer legal column should include:

* Privacy Policy
* Terms of Service
* Disclaimer

Also add this short footer disclaimer below the footer navigation:

**CrowdHarbor helps founders prepare before they raise. CrowdHarbor does not guarantee funding, act as an investor, broker, crowdfunding platform, legal advisor, tax advisor, financial advisor, or investment advisor.**

This disclaimer should appear across the full website footer, not only on legal pages.

---

# Global Legal Page Design

All three legal pages should use the same design system and layout.

Use CrowdHarbor brand colors:

* Background: `#F5F5EE`
* Text: `#000000`
* Cards: `#FFFFFF`
* Primary orange: `#FD6628`
* Muted text: `#444444`
* Border: `#E0E0E0`

The legal pages should feel:

* Professional
* Clean
* Easy to read
* Trustworthy
* Founder-friendly
* Serious but not scary

They should not look like a pasted legal document. Use proper spacing, readable typography, table of contents, content cards, and clear section headings.

---

# Legal Page Hero

Each legal page should have a simple centered hero.

Hero structure:

* Eyebrow: **CrowdHarbor legal**
* Page title
* Short supporting text
* Last updated date

No heavy 3D or complex animation on legal pages.

---

# Table of Contents

Each page should include a white table of contents card near the top.

Style:

* White background
* Rounded corners
* Border: `#E0E0E0`
* Section jump links
* Orange hover state

On mobile, the table of contents can become collapsible.

---

# Main Content Card

Legal content should appear inside a large white content card.

Style:

* Background: `#FFFFFF`
* Border: `1px solid #E0E0E0`
* Border radius: 28px
* Padding: 48–64px desktop
* Padding: 24px mobile
* Max text width around 760–860px
* Clear H2 and H3 headings
* Body text 16–18px
* Line height 1.6–1.8

Use subtle dividers between major sections.

---

# Important Notice Card

Each legal page should include this notice card:

**Important:** CrowdHarbor helps founders prepare before approaching funding sources. CrowdHarbor does not guarantee funding, act as an investor, broker, crowdfunding platform, legal advisor, tax advisor, financial advisor, or investment advisor.

Style:

* White card
* Orange left border
* Shield or info icon
* Clear text
* Calm professional tone

# Animation Direction

Keep legal page animations minimal.

Allowed:

* Hero fade in
* Table of contents fade up
* Content card fade up
* Section headings reveal subtly
* CTA card fade in

Avoid:

* 3D animation
* Floating cards
* Moving backgrounds
* Animated charts
* Heavy scroll effects
* Playful icons

Legal pages must prioritize readability and trust.

---

# Mobile Direction

Legal pages must be very readable on mobile.

Mobile requirements:

* Body text minimum 16px
* Comfortable line height
* Main content card padding reduced to 24px
* Table of contents collapsible
* No sticky side navigation
* Footer legal links stacked clearly
* Legal disclaimer readable, not tiny

---

# Accessibility Requirements

* Use proper H1, H2, and H3 hierarchy
* Strong color contrast
* Clear focus states for links
* Keyboard-accessible table of contents
* Avoid text inside images
* Avoid low-contrast gray text
* Use readable spacing between sections

---

# Final Approval Checklist

These legal pages are approved only if:

* Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Disclaimer are three separate pages
* Each page has its own route
* Footer links connect to all three pages
* Footer includes the short legal disclaimer
* Pages use the CrowdHarbor brand system
* Legal text is easy to read
* Important no-guarantee language is visible
* CrowdHarbor does not appear to be a broker, investor, or advisor
* Uploaded founder materials are addressed in the Privacy Policy
* Payment, refund, and third-party tools are addressed in Terms
* Disclaimer is direct and clear
* Mobile readability is strong
* CTAs are gentle and not overly sales-focused
