# CrowdHarbor Intake Form Page Design Direction

## Page Goal

The Intake Form page is where interest becomes action.

This page must help founders submit their company information, funding goals, current materials, and biggest preparation challenges in a way that feels professional, secure, and easy.

The intake form should not feel like a basic contact form. It should feel like the beginning of a serious funding-readiness review process.

The page should make founders feel:

“I am entering a structured preparation process. CrowdHarbor is going to review my company seriously.”

Primary goal:

**Complete founder intake submission**

Secondary goals:

* Upload pitch deck or documents
* Select the right service path
* Move to payment or application review
* Trigger internal admin workflow

---

# Main Page Message

## Core Message

**Start your funding preparation review.**

## Supporting Message

Share your company stage, traction, funding goal, materials, and biggest challenges so CrowdHarbor can review your readiness and recommend the right preparation path.

## Primary CTA

**Continue**

Final submit CTA:

**Submit My Intake**

Payment CTA if needed:

**Continue to Payment**

---

# Page Visual Style

The Intake Form page should feel:

* Premium
* Calm
* Secure
* Focused
* Professional
* Easy to complete
* Founder-friendly
* Not overwhelming

It should not feel:

* Like a long government form
* Like a cheap Typeform clone
* Like a basic contact form
* Like a complicated investor application
* Like a playful quiz

Use the brand system:

* Background: `#F5F5EE`
* Text: `#000000`
* Cards: `#FFFFFF`
* Primary orange: `#FD6628`
* Muted text: `#444444`
* Border: `#E0E0E0`

Orange should be used for:

* Progress bar
* Selected options
* Primary buttons
* Step numbers
* Upload active state
* Error focus state
* Completion state

---

# Recommended Page Structure

The Intake Form page should have these sections:

1. Intake Page Header
2. Intro / Welcome Card
3. Progress Indicator
4. Multi-Step Form
5. Side Guidance Panel
6. File Upload Section
7. Review Before Submit Step
8. Payment / Application Routing
9. Confirmation State
10. Privacy and Trust Notes

# 1. Intake Page Header

## Purpose

Keep the founder focused on the form while still making the brand feel professional.

## Header Style

Use a simplified header, not the full marketing navigation.

Desktop:

* CrowdHarbor logo left
* Small trust text right
* Optional “Need help?” link

Header right text:

**Secure founder intake**

or

**Preparation before outreach**

Avoid too many navigation links because the goal is form completion.

## Design Style

* Background: `#F5F5EE`
* Subtle bottom border: `#E0E0E0`
* Height: 72px
* Sticky only if it does not distract

## Mobile

* Logo left
* Small lock/trust icon right
* No full navigation menu needed

---

# 2. Intro / Welcome Card

## Purpose

Before asking questions, explain why the form matters and reduce friction.

## Recommended Copy

### Heading

**Let’s understand where your company stands.**

### Supporting Copy

This intake helps CrowdHarbor review your company stage, traction, current materials, funding goal, and preparation gaps before recommending the right funding-readiness path.

### Small Trust Line

Your information is used only to review your readiness and preparation needs.

## Layout Direction

Use a centered white card at the top of the page.

Inside:

* Heading
* Short explanation
* Estimated completion time
* Trust note

Estimated time:

**Takes about 7–10 minutes**

## Design Style

* White card
* Rounded corners: 24–28px
* Soft border
* Small orange icon or progress mark
* Calm and premium

## Animation

* Welcome card fades up
* Progress indicator appears after welcome card

# 3. Progress Indicator

## Purpose

Show founders where they are in the process and reduce drop-off.

## Recommended Steps

Use 7 steps:

1. Founder Details
2. Company Stage
3. Traction
4. Funding Goal
5. Materials
6. Challenges
7. Review & Submit

If payment is included directly, add:

8. Payment

## Desktop Progress Design

Use a horizontal progress bar at the top of the form card.

Each step should show:

* Step number
* Short label
* Active state
* Completed state

## Mobile Progress Design

Use a simple progress bar with text:

**Step 2 of 7: Company Stage**

Do not show all step labels on mobile if it becomes crowded.

## Visual Style

* Inactive line: `#E0E0E0`
* Active line: `#FD6628`
* Completed step: orange circle with checkmark
* Current step: black text
* Future step: muted text

## Animation

* Progress bar fills smoothly when moving to next step
* Completed step checkmark appears with subtle scale animation

# 4. Multi-Step Form Layout

## Purpose

The form should feel simple and manageable.

Do not show all questions at once. Use one section per step.

## Main Layout

Desktop:

Use a 2-column layout.

Left / main column:

* Form card

Right / side column:

* Guidance panel
* Current step explanation
* Trust notes
* What happens next

Mobile:

* Single-column
* Guidance panel appears below or is collapsed

## Form Card Style

* Background: `#FFFFFF`
* Border: `1px solid #E0E0E0`
* Border radius: 28px
* Padding: 32–48px desktop
* Padding: 24px mobile
* Soft shadow
* Clear heading
* Short helper text
* Large form fields

## Field Style

Inputs:

* White or very light background
* Border: `#E0E0E0`
* Border radius: 14–16px
* Padding: 14–16px
* Font size: 16px minimum
* Focus border: `#FD6628`
* Error border: use orange/red carefully

Labels:

* Black
* Medium weight
* Clear and simple

Helper text:

* Muted text `#444444`
* Smaller size
* Short

# 9. Step 5 — Materials

## Purpose

Collect or identify fundraising materials.

This step is important because CrowdHarbor reviews pitch decks, financial models, business plans, data room status, and other founder assets.

## Step Heading

**What materials do you already have?**

## Helper Text

Upload any current materials you use or plan to use for funding conversations. If you do not have them yet, that is okay — missing materials are part of the review.

## Question 1

### Which materials do you currently have?

Allow multiple selection.

Options:

* Pitch deck
* Financial model
* Business plan
* One-page company summary
* Product demo
* Data room documents
* Cap table
* Customer proof
* Use-of-funds breakdown
* None yet

## Question 2

### Upload documents

File upload component.

Accepted files:

* PDF
* DOC/DOCX
* PPT/PPTX
* XLS/XLSX
* CSV
* PNG/JPG if needed

## File Upload Design

Use a large drag-and-drop upload area.

Upload box copy:

**Drag your files here or click to upload**

Helper text:

**Pitch deck, financial model, business plan, company overview, or other fundraising materials.**

## Upload States

### Empty

White upload box with border.

### Hover

Border turns orange.

### Uploading

Progress bar appears.

### Uploaded

File chip appears with filename, file type, and remove option.

### Error

Clear error message appears.

## Important UX Note

Do not make file upload required.

Some founders may not have materials yet. The form should still allow them to continue.

Add option:

**I do not have these materials yet**

## Animation

* Upload box highlights on drag
* Progress bar animates
* Uploaded file chips slide in

# 10. Step 6 — Biggest Challenges

## Purpose

Understand what the founder feels most unsure about.

This step should connect emotionally and strategically.

## Step Heading

**What feels most unclear or difficult right now?**

## Helper Text

Select the areas where you need the most clarity before approaching capital.

## Challenge Options

Allow multiple selection.

* I am not sure if we are ready to raise
* Our pitch is unclear
* Our pitch deck needs work
* We do not have a financial model
* Our use of funds is not clear
* We do not know which funding path fits
* We have been rejected before
* Our traction story is weak
* Our data room is incomplete
* We need help preparing for outreach

## Follow-Up Question

### In your own words, what is your biggest funding concern?

Textarea.

Placeholder:

**Example: We have early traction but I am not sure whether we should pursue crowdfunding, angels, or grants first.**

## Design Direction

Use selectable challenge cards.

These cards should feel supportive, not judgmental.

Avoid making founders feel embarrassed about gaps.

## Animation

* Cards reveal softly
* Selected cards get orange border and checkmark
* Textarea appears after challenge selection if desired

# 11. Step 7 — Review & Submit

## Purpose

Let the founder review everything before submission.

This step should reduce anxiety and create confidence.

## Step Heading

**Review your intake before submitting.**

## Helper Text

Make sure the information looks correct. CrowdHarbor will use this to understand your readiness and preparation needs.

## Layout Direction

Use a clean summary card.

Show sections:

1. Founder details
2. Company stage
3. Traction
4. Funding goal
5. Materials
6. Challenges
7. Selected service

Each section should have:

* Section title
* Summary
* Edit button

## Selected Service

If the user entered from a specific CTA, preselect the service:

* Funding Readiness Review
* Capital Pathway Sprint
* Fundraising Preparation Program

Allow the user to change if appropriate.

## Final Consent Checkbox

Required checkbox:

**I understand CrowdHarbor provides preparation guidance and does not guarantee funding or act as an investor, broker, legal advisor, tax advisor, or investment advisor.**

Optional marketing checkbox:

**I agree to receive updates and resources from CrowdHarbor.**

## Final Button

If direct review:

**Submit My Intake**

If paid review:

**Continue to Payment**

If application-based:

**Submit Application**

## Design Style

* White summary card
* Clear section dividers
* Edit links in orange
* Consent box visible but not scary
* Final CTA button full-width or strongly visible

## Animation

* Summary sections reveal one by one
* Consent checkbox animates when selected
* Submit button activates after required consent

# 12. Payment / Application Routing

## Purpose

Route users correctly based on offer.

## Recommended Routing

### Funding Readiness Review

Direct Stripe checkout.

Flow:

Submit intake → Stripe checkout → Payment success → Confirmation page

### Capital Pathway Sprint

Recommended for launch:

Application-first.

Flow:

Submit intake → Confirmation page → Admin review → Payment link or acceptance email

Alternative:

Direct checkout if operations are ready.

### Fundraising Preparation Program

Application only.

Flow:

Submit application → Admin review → Call / custom quote / payment link

## Designer Direction

If payment is part of the intake, create a payment routing step after review.

Show a simple transition screen:

### Heading

**Your intake is ready.**

### Supporting Copy

Continue to the next step to complete your selected preparation path.

Buttons based on offer:

* Continue to Payment
* Submit Application
* Schedule Strategy Call

## Visual Style

Use a clean white transition card with a small progress indicator.

Your information is used to review your readiness and preparation needs.

# Form Interaction Details

## Buttons

Primary button:

* Background: `#FD6628`
* White text
* Rounded corners
* Strong hover
* Arrow icon optional

Secondary button:

* White or transparent
* Black text
* Border: `#E0E0E0`

Back button:

* Muted style
* Should not compete with Continue button

## Button Labels

Use clear labels:

* Continue
* Back
* Review Intake
* Submit My Intake
* Continue to Payment
* Submit Application

Avoid:

* Next if it feels too generic
* Submit too early
* Finish before final step

## Error States

Errors should be clear and human.

Examples:

* Please enter your email address.
* Please select your current company stage.
* Please choose at least one funding path or select “Not sure yet.”
* File type not supported. Please upload PDF, DOC, PPT, XLS, or image files.

## Save Progress

If possible, add auto-save or local progress.

If not possible, do not promise it.

Suggested UI if implemented:

**Progress saved locally**

# Intake Form Animation Direction

The form should feel smooth, not flashy.

## Step Transitions

* Current step fades out
* New step slides in softly
* Progress bar fills
* Guidance panel updates

## Selectable Cards

* Border turns orange
* Light background tint
* Checkmark appears
* Slight scale or lift

## File Upload

* Drag state highlight
* Upload progress bar
* File chips appear

## Review Step

* Summary cards reveal in order

## Success State

* Checkmark animates
* Confirmation message fades in

Avoid:

* Spinning 3D objects
* Excessive transitions
* Long animations that slow completion
* Confetti-heavy success state

# 3D Direction for Intake Form

Use minimal 3D on this page.

The form should be fast, clean, and focused.

Recommended 3D use:

## Welcome Area

Small 3D document/report icon or intake card stack.

## Side Panel

Very subtle floating mini-card showing:

* Readiness Review
* Pathway Fit
* Action Plan

## Confirmation State

Small 3D report card stack showing the submitted intake turning into a review.

Rules:

* No major 3D model in the form area
* No distracting floating objects while typing
* Static fallback on mobile
* Keep load speed high

# Responsive Direction

## Desktop

* Centered page layout
* Main form card left or center
* Sticky guidance panel right
* Horizontal progress indicator
* Generous spacing

## Tablet

* Form card centered
* Guidance panel can move below form heading
* Progress indicator may simplify

## Mobile

* Single-column layout
* No sticky side panel
* Progress bar: “Step X of 7”
* CTA buttons full-width
* Large tap targets
* Selectable cards stacked
* File upload simple and easy
* Review summary stacked
* Keep form fields at least 16px font size

Mobile completion is very important. The form should not feel long or intimidating.

---

# Accessibility Direction

The form must be accessible.

Rules:

* Every field needs a visible label
* Error messages must be readable
* Keyboard navigation should work
* Focus states must be visible
* Buttons should be large enough to tap
* Do not rely only on color for selection
* Use checkmarks or icons along with color
* Support reduced motion if possible
* Form should be readable with high contrast

# Intake Page Approval Checklist

The Intake Form page is approved only if:

* The form feels premium and professional
* It does not overwhelm the founder
* The process is clearly broken into steps
* Progress is visible
* The form collects all required founder information
* File upload feels easy and optional
* The founder understands what happens after submission
* Legal/trust expectations are clear
* Mobile experience is clean
* CTAs are clear
* Error states are designed
* Success state is designed
* Payment/application routing is clear
* The design uses CrowdHarbor brand colors consistently

# Final Creative Direction

The Intake Form page should visually communicate:

**This is the beginning of a serious funding-preparation review.**

The founder should feel guided through a calm, structured process where they can share their company details, materials, funding goals, and challenges without feeling overwhelmed.

By the end of the form, the founder should feel:

“I submitted the right information, CrowdHarbor understands what to review, and I know what happens next.”
