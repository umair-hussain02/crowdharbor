You are a senior professional UI/UX designer, conversion-focused web designer, and product design strategist.

Design a premium, modern, strategic **Services Page** for a company called **CrowdHarbor**.

CrowdHarbor helps early-stage founders prepare before fundraising by diagnosing readiness, reviewing fundraising materials, identifying blockers, recommending the right capital pathway, and helping founders build a stronger preparation plan before approaching investors, crowdfunding platforms, grants, accelerators, or other capital sources.

The Services Page must communicate this core idea:

**Choose the preparation path that fits your funding stage.**

The page should help founders clearly understand which CrowdHarbor offer fits their current stage and level of preparation.

The page must not feel like a cheap pricing page, generic agency services page, basic AI quiz, crowdfunding platform, investor marketplace, or simple automated score tool.

It should feel like a premium funding-preparation company offering a structured journey from diagnosis to review, pathway recommendation, and action planning.

# 18. FAQ Section

## Purpose

Answer service-selection objections.

## Section Copy

Heading:

**Questions before choosing a service.**

Use accordion FAQ cards.

## FAQ Items

1. **Which service should I start with?**
   If you are unsure where you stand, start with the Funding Readiness Review. If you are preparing to raise soon and need deeper review, the Capital Pathway Sprint is the best starting point.

2. **Is the Capital Pathway Sprint the main offer?**
   Yes. It is designed for founders who need readiness diagnosis, material review, pathway strategy, and a practical roadmap before outreach.

3. **Do I need traction to use CrowdHarbor?**
   CrowdHarbor is best for founders who have started a company and have some product, traction, users, revenue, or market proof. Very early idea-stage founders may still benefit from a readiness review, but the strongest fit is founders preparing for funding.

4. **Do you write my pitch deck for me?**
   The services focus on review, structure, narrative improvement, and preparation guidance. Full pitch deck creation can be added later or handled as a custom support option if needed.

5. **Do you guarantee funding?**
   No. CrowdHarbor helps founders prepare before approaching capital. It does not guarantee funding outcomes.

6. **Are you an investor or broker?**
   No. CrowdHarbor is not an investor, broker, crowdfunding platform, or funding guarantor.

7. **Can you introduce me to funding platforms or partners?**
   CrowdHarbor may recommend suitable pathways or partner platforms where appropriate, but this should never be positioned as guaranteed access or guaranteed funding.

8. **What happens after I choose a service?**
   You complete the intake, submit your company details and materials, and CrowdHarbor reviews your readiness, gaps, materials, and pathway fit based on the selected service.

Open animation: height + opacity

FAQ behavior:
