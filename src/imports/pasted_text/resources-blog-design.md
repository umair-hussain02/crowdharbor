# CrowdHarbor Resources / Blog Page Design Direction

## Page Goal

The Resources page should position CrowdHarbor as an educational authority for early-stage founders preparing to raise capital.

This page is not only a blog. It should feel like a founder preparation library.

The goal is to help founders understand:

* How to prepare before fundraising
* What investors, crowdfunding platforms, grants, and accelerators may review
* What materials they need
* How to improve readiness
* How to choose the right capital pathway
* Why preparation matters before outreach

The Resources page should support three business goals:

1. Build trust before purchase
2. Improve SEO and organic traffic
3. Educate founders before and after intake submission

Primary CTA:

**Check Your Funding Readiness**

Secondary CTA:

**View Sample Report**

---

# Main Page Message

## Core Message

**Founder resources for preparing before you raise.**

## Supporting Message

Explore practical guides, checklists, and funding-readiness insights to help you understand what is weak, what is missing, and what to fix before approaching capital.

---

# Page Visual Style

The Resources page should feel:

* Educational
* Premium
* Clear
* Founder-friendly
* Strategic
* Organized
* Trustworthy
* Easy to browse

It should not feel like:

* A generic blog page
* A news website
* A messy article archive
* A basic SEO content dump
* A casual personal blog

Use the brand system:

* Background: `#F5F5EE`
* Text: `#000000`
* Cards: `#FFFFFF`
* Primary orange: `#FD6628`
* Muted text: `#444444`
* Border: `#E0E0E0`

Use orange for:

* Category labels
* Featured article accent
* CTA buttons
* Active filters
* Important resource tags
* Hover states

The designer should also prepare a reusable article page template.

## Article Page Structure

1. Article Header
2. Author / Reading Time
3. Main Article Content
4. Key Takeaway Box
5. Related Resources
6. CTA Box
7. Disclaimer Note

## Article Header

Include:

* Category pill
* Article title
* Short intro
* Reading time
* Date if needed

## Content Layout

Desktop:

* Main article column: 720–800px
* Optional right sidebar:

  * Table of contents
  * CTA card
  * Related guide

Mobile:

* Single column
* Sidebar moves below content or is hidden

## Article Content Style

Use strong readability:

* H2 sections
* Short paragraphs
* Bullet lists
* Pull quotes
* Checklist boxes
* Example cards
* CTA boxes

Use 3D lightly.

## Hero

A stack of resource cards or guide documents.

# Resources Page Animation Direction

Use subtle educational-site motion.

## Page Load

* Hero text fades up
* Resource stack appears
* CTA buttons reveal

## Scroll

* Featured article card fades up
* Category filters appear
* Article cards reveal in stagger
* Checklist items animate in groups
* Newsletter card fades in

## Hover

* Article cards lift
* Category pill changes border
* CTA arrows move
* Template cards slightly tilt or lift

Avoid:

* Too much motion on article grids
* Heavy animations that hurt readability
* Flashy blog effects

# Responsive Direction

## Desktop

* Centered hero
* Featured resource large card
* 3-column article grid
* Checklist section 2-column
* Popular guides 2x2 or 4-column
* Newsletter card wide

## Tablet

* Article grid 2-column
* Checklist can remain 2-column
* Popular guides 2-column

## Mobile

* Hero text left-aligned or centered
* CTA buttons full-width
* Category filters horizontally scrollable
* Article grid single-column
* Checklist single-column
* Newsletter form stacked
* 3D simplified or hidden

Mobile should feel like a premium reading experience.

* Calm

* Honest

* Preparation
* Readiness
* Materials
* Funding blockers
* Pathway fit
* Founder clarity

# Resources Page Approval Checklist

The Resources page is approved only if:

* It feels like a founder preparation library, not a generic blog
* Categories are easy to browse
* Featured article is clear and useful
* Article cards are clean and premium
* Page supports SEO
* CTAs naturally lead to the intake form
* Downloadable templates are not fake if unavailable
* Mobile reading experience is clean
* Brand colors are consistent
* The page reinforces preparation before outreach
* Legal/disclaimer language is present where needed

The Resources page should visually communicate:
