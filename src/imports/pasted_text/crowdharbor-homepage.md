You are a senior professional UI/UX designer and conversion-focused web designer. Design a premium, modern, strategic homepage for a company called CrowdHarbor.

CrowdHarbor helps early-stage founders prepare before fundraising. The website must communicate this core idea immediately:

“Your funding journey should not start with outreach. It should start with preparation.”

The homepage should make founders understand that CrowdHarbor helps them diagnose funding readiness, identify weak points, review fundraising materials, choose the right capital pathway, and build an action plan before approaching investors, crowdfunding platforms, grants, accelerators, or other capital sources.

The design must feel like a premium funding-readiness and capital-pathway advisory service, not a playful SaaS quiz, generic agency website, crowdfunding platform, investor marketplace, or basic AI tool.

18. FAQ Section

Section heading:

Questions founders ask before starting.

Create accordion FAQ cards.

FAQ items:

Is CrowdHarbor a fundraising broker?
No. CrowdHarbor helps founders prepare before approaching funding sources. We do not guarantee funding or act as a broker.
Do you guarantee that I will raise money?
No. The goal is to help you understand readiness, fix gaps, and prepare stronger materials before outreach.
What if I do not have a pitch deck yet?
You can still start. CrowdHarbor can help identify what materials are missing and what should be prepared first.
Which funding paths do you review?
Crowdfunding, angel investors, grants, accelerators, pre-seed, revenue-based financing, and strategic partner readiness.
Is this only for tech startups?
No. It is for early-stage founders with a business, traction, product, revenue, or market proof who are thinking about funding.
What happens after I submit the intake?
Your company information and materials are reviewed. You then receive a readiness diagnosis, pathway recommendation, and next-step action plan depending on your selected offer.
Is the report automated?
The experience should feel like a professional review, not just an automated score. The score is only part of the preparation process.
