# Task: Create CrowdHarbor Contact / Partner Inquiry Page and Link It in Footer

Please create a dedicated **Contact / Partner Inquiry page** for CrowdHarbor.

This page should not replace the founder intake form. The Contact page is for questions, support, partnerships, workshops, and general inquiries. Founders who are ready for a readiness review or Sprint should be guided to the founder intake page.

---

# Required Page Route

Create the page at:

`/contact`

Footer link:

Contact → `/contact`

Also link this page from any “Contact CrowdHarbor” text links across the website.

# 8. FAQ Section

## Section Heading

**Contact questions.**

Use accordion cards.

FAQ items:

### Should I use the contact form or the founder intake form?

Use the founder intake form if you want CrowdHarbor to review your company readiness, materials, funding goal, and preparation gaps. Use the contact form for questions, partnerships, workshops, or support.

### Can I ask a question before paying?

Yes. You can use the contact form for general questions before choosing a service.

### Can accelerators or universities contact CrowdHarbor?

Yes. Startup communities, universities, accelerators, and founder programs can inquire about workshops or group preparation sessions.

### Can I send my pitch deck through the contact form?

The founder intake form is the better place to submit business materials for review. Only attach a file through the contact form if it is needed for your inquiry.

### How quickly will CrowdHarbor respond?

Use flexible copy unless response time is finalized: **CrowdHarbor reviews inquiries as soon as possible and responds based on inquiry type.**

### Do you provide investor introductions?

CrowdHarbor focuses on preparation before outreach. Any partner or pathway guidance should not be understood as guaranteed investor access or guaranteed funding.

### Do you guarantee funding?

No. CrowdHarbor helps founders prepare before approaching capital. It does not guarantee funding outcomes.

### Are you an investor or broker?

No. CrowdHarbor is not an investor, broker, crowdfunding platform, funding guarantor, legal advisor, tax advisor, or investment advisor.
